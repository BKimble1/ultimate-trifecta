# Season Champion contest: rules (owner review needed)

The game shows these rules from the champion poster and the Rankings screen
(`ChampionPromo.rules_text()`, `game/src/ui/champion_promo.gd`). App Store
Review Guideline 5.3 requires that a contest is sponsored by the developer,
that its official rules are in the app, and that the rules say Apple is not a
sponsor or involved. **These are a draft written for the owner: please have
them checked for your country before the first season ends**, and change the
text in `champion_promo.gd` (the test `test_rankings` checks the required
statements stay in it).

What the owner has to do each season:
1. When the season ends (every 30 days from 2026-12-01: Season 1 runs
   2026-12-01 to 2026-12-30 UTC; the November preseason has no leaderboards
   and no champion), open App Store Connect › the app › Game
   Center › Leaderboards › Season Points and look at the top entries of the
   finished occurrence.
2. Remove any score you believe was cheated (App Store Connect can delete a
   player's score), then take the #1.
3. Announce the winner's Game Center name (support page; a later build can
   show it in the poster) and wait up to 14 days for them to contact you.
4. Make the outfit and ship it as the tier 100 reward of a following season.

Rules text (as shown in the game):

1. Sponsor. This contest is run by Idlery Games, the developer of Ultimate Trifecta. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this contest in any way.
2. No purchase necessary. Entry is free: you take part by playing online rounds. Buying anything in the game does not improve your chances.
3. Season. Each season runs 30 days (00:00 UTC on its first day to 23:59 UTC on its last). Each season is a separate contest.
4. How the winner is decided. The player at #1 on the Season Points leaderboard in Game Center when the season ends. Season Points come only from online rounds with at least one other real player. Before announcing a winner, the developer reviews the top scores and removes any it reasonably believes were cheated, made with a modified game, or earned against these rules; the next eligible player then moves up.
5. Eligibility. Open to players with a Game Center account who are 13 or older (younger players need a parent or guardian's permission). Employees of the developer and their households can't win. Void where prohibited by law.
6. The prize. The winner may send the developer a concept (a drawing, a description or both) and a name for a cosmetic outfit. The developer will make an outfit inspired by it, as the tier 100 reward of a following season's Season Pass, adapting it to the game's style and content rules (suitable for all ages, not copying anyone else's work, brand or likeness). No cash value; not transferable or exchangeable. Cosmetic only.
7. Contact. The developer announces the winner's Game Center name in the game and on its support page; the winner contacts the developer through the support page within 14 days, or the next eligible player is chosen.
8. Rights. By sending a concept the winner confirms it is their own original work and allows the developer to use, adapt and publish it in the game and its promotion, with credit to the winner's chosen name.
9. General. The developer may cancel or change the contest if it can't run as planned (for example because of cheating or a technical failure), and will say so in the game.
