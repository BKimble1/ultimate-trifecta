1.6 internal beta (V7: Pause, straighter running, compact Locker/Pass/Friends/Settings, goggles, louder music). Please tell us your iPhone model:

- Pause: while steering with one thumb, tap Pause with another finger. The menu should open every time, and Resume, the camera slider, Reduced motion and Leave match should all take normal taps. Try it many times in a row.
- Practice: Pause really stops the round (clock, bots, everyone). Resume carries on from the same moment, with no jump. Putting the app in the background also pauses Practice.
- Party rounds: the menu says "Online match continues." and the round keeps going for everyone (you can still be caught).
- Leave match asks first and says what happens (as host it ends the party for everyone). Stay goes back; Leave leaves once.
- Running straight: push the stick straight up and hold, from the middle of the left side and from near the bottom-left corner. You should run straight; the view should not drift round. Small sideways thumb wobble should not steer. Deliberate turns, diagonals, strafing and reverse should still feel normal. Lift your thumb: you stop.
- Season Pass: both the Free and the Premium rows are fully visible at every tier, with clear reward pictures. The service and App Store products aren't set up in this build, so rewards say they're unavailable. That's expected; please don't try to buy anything.
- Locker and Emotes: compact cards, emote icons centred in their circles, a small Equipped mark.
- Friends and Settings: Create Party and Join are visible straight away; settings rows are compact. Check the code field with the keyboard open.
- Characters: goggles should sit properly on every runner.
- Music is a little louder and should still loop with no gap or click (about every 34 s).
- If running ever curves or Pause misbehaves, turn on Settings > Diagnostics (beta), play, then Share summary. It records the stick and camera numbers, but no names or codes.
