2.2 (35): fairer bots and fixes from your playtest.

- Night Watch bots are slower to react and only chase someone they see. Dive into a bush where they can't see you and they walk past; if they saw you go in, they check that bush.
- The round no longer ends early while a person is still running.
- As Night Watch, runner bots are easier to catch. The Night Watch has no Boost.
- Runners can drive an empty golf cart (Drive button next to it). A runner's cart can't tag or knock anyone.
- Squeaky Duck: throw it near the Night Watch to daze them for 2.5 s; it then squeaks and draws the bots.
- The portal by the Bell Tower is fixed.
- Coins you pick up in a round now reach your wallet, for friends too. Tap +XP or +Coins on the results to see the breakdown.
- Smoother start of an online round (no jump when the round appears).
- Tell us if the bots now feel too easy.
