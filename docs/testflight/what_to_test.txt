2.2: the lobby is home, Quick Match, Rankings, chat. The game service still isn't connected, so purchases, Coins and Season rewards say they're unavailable; please don't try to buy anything. Tell us your iPhone or iPad model:

- Home is now your party lobby: walk around (Walk), invite friends (Invite friends / Friends / Share), and press PLAY. Tap the playlist card to choose Quick Match (random players online, bots fill in), Party vs Bots or Practice.
- Quick Match: two testers press PLAY at the same time; you should land in one match. Alone, it searches for up to 25 s, then starts with bots.
- Friends: invite a Game Center friend from home; they join your lobby and you play together. Try "Add friends" (Game Center's friend request screen).
- Chat: in a party, open Chat and type. Bad words should come out bleeped (#%&*); links, phone numbers and threats are refused. Tell us anything that slips through.
- Names: change your name in Settings › Profile; rude names are refused, and your name shows to friends in a party.
- Round start: keep your thumb on the stick through the countdown; you should move the instant it says GO. The role card is now a small banner so you can see your runner. Tell us if anything stutters when the round begins.
- Loading tips rotate while a round loads.
- Rankings (gold button, top right): your stats now; the Season Points and Fastest Run leaderboards (Game Center) open with Season 1 on 1 December. The Season Champion poster also pops up on launch now and then (X to close).
- After a round: your run time, personal best and Season Points (online rounds with another real player count).
- Buttons have a new glossy look. Tell us if any text is cut off or hard to read on iPhone or iPad.
