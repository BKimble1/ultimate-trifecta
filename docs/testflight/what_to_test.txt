1.9 internal beta (pass 9: one steady full speed, Season Pass to tier 100 with two new skins, better-fitting outfits). Please tell us your iPhone model:

- Running: push the stick all the way and hold it. You keep one steady full speed for as long as you hold it. There is no Sprint button, meter or recharge any more. A gentle push walks (silent), a part push jogs (quieter). On a controller or keyboard: left stick all the way, or WASD. Tell us if the speed ever dips or pulses, or if full speed is hard to reach with your thumb.
- Night Watch: you are a little faster than a runner on foot, so on a straight you close in slowly. Press Tag when it lights up. Tell us if chases feel too long or too short, and who wins most rounds.
- Dive: unchanged. One dive per jump, then a short landing.
- Turbo Sneakers are now a real escape for 3 seconds. Tell us if they feel too strong.
- Season Pass: now 100 tiers. Your Season XP, claims and Premium are kept. Try "You're at", "Next reward" and the 30, 50 and 100 shortcuts above the track. Record Breaker is the Premium reward at tier 50 and Dr. Doom at tier 100. Open them to preview.
- Outfits: watch for clothes floating off the body, gaps, or anything poking through while running, turning, jumping, diving and splashing. A screenshot helps.
- Online: everyone in a party needs 1.9. Older versions are asked to update.
- The game service and App Store products are still not set up, so buying, claiming and challenge progress say they're unavailable in this build. That's expected; please don't try to buy anything.
