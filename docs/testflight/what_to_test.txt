2.2 (32): parties stay together, Dunk and Block. Sign in to Game Center first. Two phones in one party (Friends > Invite > Create party, then Join with code):

- Ready: everyone taps Ready, the leader too, in any order. A check mark shows next to each ready name. The leader's Start lights up when all are ready.
- Tap a friend's card > Promote to leader. The crown moves; the new leader starts the next round.
- Roles change at random every round (not always the same person on Night Watch).
- In a round, a guest taps pause > Leave: you go back to the lobby and stay in the party. Friends still playing show as blue holograms. When their round ends you play the next one together. The leader's Leave asks to end the round for everyone.
- Leave party (top right of the lobby) is the only way out of a party. The code is under Friends > Invite.
- Walk with the basketball: the camera stays on you. Near the hoop, Shoot turns into Dunk. Next to a friend who is shooting, tap Block.
- Change your name in Settings: the name stays above the keyboard.
- Tell us any error message word for word (a screenshot helps).
