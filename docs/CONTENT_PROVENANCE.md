# Content provenance for the App Store submission

Blake Kimble confirmed on 22 November 2026:

- Record Breaker and Dr. Doom are original skins made for Ultimate Trifecta,
  cleared for commercial use, including any people they portray. They do not
  copy a third-party protected character. Dr. Doom is the project's original
  suited character.
- Blake made the game's music and confirmed that the creation tools' terms
  permit commercial use in the app. The music is not an imported commercial
  recording by another artist.

This supersedes the unresolved content-rights assumptions in older release
notes. Do not describe these skins or tracks as someone else's copyrighted
content, or ask the owner to clear them again without new evidence.

Original work is not the same as public-domain or "copyright-free" work.
This records the owner's statement; it is not an independent legal opinion,
a trademark clearance, or a promise of App Review approval. Technical source
and processing details remain in ASSET_LICENSES.md. Open-source engine and
font notices must remain intact.

For App Store Connect, these original skins and owner-created tracks alone
are not a reason to switch the content-rights answer to third-party content.
Keep the declaration accurate to everything actually bundled in the app.

Suggested review clarification, if Apple asks:

> The character skins are original designs created for Ultimate Trifecta.
> The game's music was created by developer Blake Kimble. We have the rights
> needed for commercial use of these assets in the app and its promotion.
