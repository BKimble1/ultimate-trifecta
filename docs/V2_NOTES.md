# V2 implementation notes

These are short notes for whoever works on the V2 changes next. Evidence and test results are in [TEST_REPORT.md](../TEST_REPORT.md).

## Render path and clarity (G1)

**What was measured.**
- At 2532×1170, the root viewport renders 3D at the full window resolution: `root_render_px` = window, `scaling_3d_scale` 1.0.
- The blur in the V1 wardrobe and lobby previews came from the stretched `SubViewportContainer`. It sizes its SubViewport in canvas units, so the wardrobe rendered 400×470 px and was shown at 650×764 px, a 1.63× upscale. The lobby rendered 990×436 px and was shown at 1609×708 px.
- The `Preview3D.new(Vector2i(480, 300))` constant in the source was not the cause; the container overrode it.

**The fix: one sizing strategy.**
- Full-screen menu scenes (home, wardrobe, lobby, results) are a 3D stage, `DormStage`, rendered in the root viewport. There is no resampling.
- `Preview3D` (inline previews only) renders its SubViewport at *displayed pixel size* and draws it 1:1 through a TextureRect. Nothing else resizes it.

**Diagnostics** live in `game/src/dev/diag.gd`, excluded from exports.
- Turn them on with `--diag` (overlay) or `--diag-report=path` (JSON every 5 s).
- They log window and canvas sizes, root render size and 3D scale, MSAA/SSAA/TAA, renderer and adapter, every SubViewport's render size against its displayed size, frame-time percentiles, draw calls, primitives and memory.

**Presets.** `QualityPreset` (`src/view/quality.gd`) offers two fixed presets. They are never switched automatically.

| | Standard | Battery Saver |
|---|---|---|
| 3D resolution | 100% | 80% (bilinear) |
| MSAA | 2× | 2× |
| Moon shadows | 2 splits, 70 m, 2048 atlas | 1 split, 30 m, 1024 atlas; only characters and carts cast |
| Glow | gentle | off |
| Mesh LOD threshold | 1.0 | 3.0 |
| Frame cap | 60 fps | 30 fps |

- UI is canvas-rendered at native resolution in both presets.
- TAA, FSR, motion blur, depth of field, chromatic aberration and grain are all off.
- 4× MSAA was not adopted, because comparing it needs measured GPU headroom on a device.

**UV2.** World meshes use UV2 for per-vertex emission (x) and foliage sway (y), so it is *not* a lightmap channel and no lightmaps are baked. Lighting is dynamic: moonlight with shadows, ambient light, lamp glow pools.

**Visibility.** Tree-canopy chunks stop drawing at 230 m in Standard and 150 m in Battery Saver, inside the fog, with a hard cut and no dithered fade. Imported character meshes get Godot's automatic LODs. Distant characters advance their animation at a third of the rate.

**Colour path, measured.**
- Blender writes glTF `COLOR_0` linear and Godot keeps it, so the character shader uses vertex colour without conversion.
- Colours authored in code for `MeshKit` meshes are sRGB, and `world_common` converts them.
- `source_color` uniforms are converted by Godot.

## Characters (G2)

`tools/character/README.md` covers the pipeline, conventions and budgets. In short:
- **One asset everywhere.** A single skinned GLB, generated by a Blender `bpy` script, is used in the lobby, wardrobe, home, gameplay and results.
- **Forward convention.** At yaw 0 the character faces −Z (sim and camera). Menus call `face_toward()`.
- **Cosmetics unchanged.** Cosmetic IDs and the 5-byte network encoding are unchanged; parts are toggled per outfit, hat and shoes.
- **The `prev_vel` bug is fixed.** Acceleration now uses the previous frame's velocity, and the head spring is sub-stepped (`test_motion`).

## Motion pipeline and camera (G4)

**Order of work each frame:** input → fixed 60 Hz sim/prediction (`_physics_process`) → `_capture_tick()` stores each player's and cart's tick state → `_process` renders `lerp(prev, cur, physics fraction)` for characters, carts and the camera anchor → animation → camera.

**What changed.**
- `CharacterView` no longer smooths position. The V1 `delta*30` lerp and yaw lerp are gone, so interpolation happens exactly once.
- Discontinuities are not interpolated: the previous state is set equal to the current one and the view's history is reset (springs, velocity, nightcap). They are:
  - respawn after capture;
  - resurfacing from a splash;
  - cart entry and exit;
  - recovery, reconnect jumps, and any jump over 2.5 m.
  
  The camera cuts on these and on spectator switches.

**Camera** (`FollowCamera`).
- Manual look is applied immediately.
- All damping uses `1 − e^(−dt/τ)`, so it does not depend on frame rate.
- Horizontal follow is exact; only the vertical anchor is lightly damped (τ 0.06 s).
- Recentering is gradual, and only while the stick points mostly forward, so holding a diagonal does not make the camera circle.
- Collision:
  - a sphere sweep from the pivot, falling back to a sweep from the chest when the pivot is inside geometry;
  - the collision distance always wins over the preferred minimum;
  - pull-in is immediate; push-out waits for a short clear period (hysteresis).
- No speed zoom and no random shake; haptics replace shake on mobile, and respect the setting.

## Touch (G4)

`TouchRouter` (`src/ui/touch_router.gd`) holds the pure ownership logic and is unit-tested in `tests/test_touch_input.gd`.

**Ownership.**
- Each pointer gets one owner when it goes down.
- Buttons and reserved HUD regions (pause, minimap) are tested before the stick or camera.
- Exactly one pointer owns movement; a second finger in the stick zone becomes a camera drag.
- Holds are per pointer.

**Cancellation.**
- When a button disappears (cart exit while holding Gas, role or state change), its pointer becomes "none" and the hold is released.
- Everything is cancelled on focus loss, backgrounding, pause, scene exit, or a controller taking over.

**Stick.**
- Radial dead zone of 12% of the radius, remapped. Magnitude is kept, so sneaking works, and diagonals get no boost.
- The dynamic stick's spawn point is clamped to the screen; a fixed stick is optional.
- Sprint at the stick edge uses hysteresis: on at 0.88, off at 0.76. Hold-to-sprint is optional.

**Press edges.** Touch presses are queued, and each sim tick consumes one, so a fast jump-then-dive is never merged into a single press.

**Camera drag.**
- It uses `InputEventScreenDrag.screen_relative`: unscaled pixels, divided by the screen scale to get points.
- `TOUCH_RAD_PER_PT` is 0.0065 rad per point, multiplied by sensitivity.
- It is a displacement, never multiplied by delta.

**Settings:** sensitivity, fixed or dynamic stick, sprint mode, button size, standard or mirrored layout, haptics, reduced motion, reset, and "Try in Practice".

## Menus (G3)

**Shared theme.**
- Palette: navy #11192B, slate #223049, ivory #F4F2EC, amber #FFC668 for the one primary action per screen, teal #6FD8CC for accents and focus.
- Fredoka at weights 500, 650 and 700.
- Radii 14, 20 and 26.
- Pressed feedback is a 4% scale; the focus ring is teal.
- Transitions run 150–220 ms as a fade plus scale settle. They are layout-safe and become a fade only with Reduced Motion.

**Touch targets.**
- `UIKit.touch_min()` is 44 pt expressed in canvas units: about 81 units on an @3x phone in landscape.
- Desktop evidence runs pass `--emulate-phone` so the same rule applies.

**Party lobby.**
- Eight fixed party cells are updated in place.
- Stage characters are keyed by player uid. Arrivals hop in once and leavers are removed; outfit changes apply in place with no rebuild.
- Eight fixed marks in staggered rows, with the local player front and centre. The lobby camera stands 3.1 m high, so faces in the back row show over the front row's caps. `test_lobby` checks on screen that every face is visible.
- Bot fill is explained once, under Start ("You + 7 bots").
- Mute and Remove are in a per-player popover.

## World and HUD (G5)

**Water.**
- Deep blue to teal by distance from the shore, fine ripples, and a thin foam shoreline.
- An active target shows only a slim pulsing ring inside the shore in the water's own colour, plus its beacon.
- A splash sends one ripple out from the splash point. A stamped target's ring calms away.
- The surface itself never glows.

**Foliage.** The 4×4 Bayer dissolve is gone. Two things replace it:
- **Parting.** Canopy vertices inside a 2.6 m sphere around the camera are pushed outward, so a canopy the camera passes through never fills the screen.
- **See-through.** Canopy that lies between the camera and the followed character is cut away inside a circle around the character, 1.25 m on foot and 2 m in a cart, measured in the camera's view. The edge is clean, with no dither. It applies only in the camera pass, so tree shadows still fall on the character. Found in the cart capture, where a canopy hid the player's own cart. Only the canopy material uses `discard`.

**Props.**
- Carts are rebuilt as merged meshes, about 7 draw calls instead of about 25. Tyres and hubs, a seat and a steering wheel that match the driver rig (hands IK'd onto the rim), and lights.
- Doors, benches, lamps and tree canopies are refined.
- Layout, collision and navigation are unchanged.

**HUD.**
- Timer and home count chip at the top centre.
- Objective chips at the top left, each with a bearing arrow and distance. They collapse to "Head back to the dorm" once all three are splashed; the compass strip and banner are gone.
- Compact minimap and pause icon at the top right.
- Event feed limited to 3 lines for 4 s.
- Player name labels fade out beyond 30 m, are hidden within 3.5 m of the camera, are depth-tested (never seen through walls), and keep the "BOT" tag.

**Results.** The order is outcome, then your contribution, then rewards, with one primary action (Rematch or Play again) and the scoreboard in a drawer. Your own character is on the stage, celebrating or shrugging.
