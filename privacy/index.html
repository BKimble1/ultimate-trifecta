Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026. This policy covers the game and this website.

1. Who we are

Ultimate Trifecta is developed by Idlery Games, part of Idlery Services LLC (we, us). Contact us at support@idlery.com for privacy questions. This policy describes the intended online release and its TestFlight environment. Features and data collection depend on which services are enabled.

2. Information we process

You can play offline practice without signing in. Device settings, control preferences and local progress are stored on your device. When you use online features, we may process:

  • Game Center identity: your verified team player identifier, game profile ID, chosen name and tag, name history and account status.
  • Gameplay: your character's appearance, item ownership, match and leaderboard results, Coins ledger, Season Pass and challenge progress, timestamps and gameplay interactions.
  • Purchase records: Apple transaction identifiers, product identifiers, transaction environment and delivered entitlements. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your card details.
  • Social features: party codes, membership and timestamps, hashed identifiers of authorized Game Center friends, short-lived presence, invitations, and block relationships.
  • Safety and chat: player names and typed messages are checked for prohibited content. Ordinary chat is not retained by the game service; messages may be retained when reported. Reports can include the reporter, target, reason, details, quoted message and other context.
  • Technical and support information: session and rate-limit data, hosting request logs (which may include IP addresses), and information you choose to send us, including your email, screenshots or an optional diagnostic summary.

The game is designed without third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not request precise location, access to your personal contacts, photos or microphone for normal gameplay. Game Center friends are separate from your device contacts.

3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate players, run multiplayer and parties, display scores and mutual-friend status, save progress, verify purchases and prevent duplicate grants, enforce game rules, investigate reports, respond to support requests, secure the service and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, our legal bases include performing our agreement with you, legitimate interests in security and service operation, consent where required and compliance with law.

4. Who receives information

Apple provides Game Center, leaderboards, invitations, the App Store and payment verification. See Apple's privacy policy. Our planned game service uses Cloudflare Workers and D1 for hosting and storage; Cloudflare may process network and request information under its privacy policy. In online matches the host's device and other players receive information needed to play, including player names, appearance, match activity and chat. Mutual friends can see your presence when enabled. Game Center leaderboard entries are visible according to your Game Center settings. We may publish an eligible contest winner's Game Center name as explained in the official rules. We may disclose information to comply with law, protect safety, or prevent abuse. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. How long we keep information

Profile, purchase and progress information is kept while your profile is active and as needed to operate the game. Friend hashes are removed after 30 days without refresh or when friends access is revoked. Presence expires about 60 seconds after the last heartbeat. Invitations expire after five minutes and resolved invitations are cleared after 24 hours. Closed rooms are cleared after 24 hours. Ordinary chat is checked in real time and is not stored by our game service unless reported. Moderation records, support requests and security logs may be retained as necessary to investigate abuse, resolve requests, comply with law and protect the service. Hosting providers may have separate log and backup retention.

6. Your choices and deleting data

Go to Settings, Profile, Delete Game Profile in the app. Follow the confirmation prompts. With the online service connected, this removes your game profile, identity link, name history, friends, blocks, presence, invitations, wallet, entitlements, progress and quoted text of reported messages you sent. It also clears local profile data. Records needed to verify App Store purchases and refunds and prevent duplicate grants are retained without the profile link. Reports you filed may remain without the reporter link, and open reports against your deleted profile are closed. Apple-managed Game Center data and purchases are not deleted by this action; manage those separately through Apple.

You can also disable presence using Show when I'm playing, change Game Center permissions, or contact us to request access, correction, deletion or other rights available in your jurisdiction. See Privacy choices. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Children and international users

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information without legally required parental consent. Parents or guardians can contact us if they believe a child has provided information. Online features may also be limited by Apple's family and Game Center settings. Personal information may be processed in the United States or other locations where Apple, our hosting providers or service operators operate. Applicable privacy rights depend on where you live.

8. This website and security

This website does not use advertising cookies, behavioral analytics or an account form. Its hosting provider may process routine request information for delivery and security. Email links open your own email app. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet service is completely secure.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as features or legal requirements change. We will post the updated date here and provide additional notice where required. For questions or rights requests, email support@idlery.com.
