How a round plays

Four minutes. One way home.

  1. 01

    Sneak out

    Every round starts inside your dorm. At GO the doors are open and everyone runs for it.

  2. 02

    Splash three

    Three of the campus waters are lit tonight. Fountains, ponds, a lagoon. Jump into each one, in any order you like.

  3. 03

    Dodge the Night Watch

    They're on foot and in golf carts, and one tag puts you out. Duck into a bush, grab Speed Sneakers or take a rift to the far side of campus.

  4. 04

    Race home

    With all three splashes, get back through any door of your dorm. Inside is safe. Get enough runners home and your whole side wins.

Runners

Run.

  • Splash into all three of tonight's waters, then get home.
  • Hide in a bush and let the Night Watch walk right past.
  • Power up with Speed Sneakers, a Squeaky Duck or an Invisibility Cloak.
Night Watch

Chase.

  • Drive a golf cart across the lawns, hop out and tag.
  • Read the splash markers. They show where a runner just was.
  • Hold the line. Stop enough runners getting home and the round is yours.
Play together

Bring the whole floor.

Start a party and invite your Game Center friends, or share a six-character code. Quick Match finds players online. Empty seats are filled by bots, and they're always labelled.

  • PartiesUp to eight players. One to three Night Watch. Series of one, three or five rounds.
