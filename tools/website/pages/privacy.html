Who we are

Ultimate Trifecta is made by Idlery Games ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Ultimate Trifecta app for iPhone and iPad, the online service the game uses (the "game service") and this website. We are responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

At a glance

  • There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no analytics tools and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • We never see your payment details. Apple handles every payment.
  • Typed chat is filtered in real time and isn't stored unless someone reports it.
  • Your Game Center friends list is uploaded only as scrambled codes, never as readable IDs.
  • You can delete your game profile from inside the game at any time.

On your device

Your settings, level, stats, the look of your runner and your Coins balance as last seen are saved on your device so the game can start quickly and work offline. Practice rounds against bots happen entirely on your device. If you turn on Settings › Display › Diagnostics, a performance summary is kept on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it yourself.

The game also remembers which of our two service environments your copy uses (the App Store one, or the test one used by TestFlight and App Review). To work this out it looks only at the name of your App Store receipt file, never at its contents.

What the game service keeps

When you play online, buy something or use Friends, the game talks to our game service. It stores the following, linked to your game profile:

InformationWhat it is
ProfileAn internal profile ID, your player name and its #tag, your past names, the items your runner wears, your account status (active or suspended) and timestamps.
Game Center linkYour Game Center player ID for this game, checked with Apple's signature, so only you can use your profile.
ProgressSeason progress and claimed rewards, challenge progress and the results of online rounds that paid rewards, so nothing is paid twice.
Wallet and purchasesYour Coins balance and history, the items you own, and for App Store purchases the transaction ID, product and what it delivered. Never payment or card details.
FriendsYour Game Center friends list as keyed hashes (scrambled codes we can't turn back into IDs), so the game can show which mutual friends are playing and deliver invites. Up to 500 friends.
Playing statusWhether you're online, in a party or in a round, the party, and your game version. Shown only to mutual friends, never during a round, and you can turn it off with "Show when I'm playing" in Friends.
InvitationsWho invited whom to which party, and whether it was accepted.
PartiesParty codes, the host and members, and the party's state.
BlocksThe players you've blocked.
ReportsReports you send or that are sent about you: reason, optional details, context and the player's name at the time. For a reported chat message, that message.
Safety recordsShort-lived counters that limit spam and abuse, signed-out sessions, and a log of actions our moderators take.

Typed chat is checked by the filter on each device and by the game service as it's sent. Messages aren't stored. Only a message that someone reports is kept, with the report.

We don't collect your email address, phone number, location, contacts outside Game Center, photos, microphone, browsing history, advertising data or device identifiers.

Apple and Game Center

The game uses Game Center to sign you in, find your friends, send invites and run matches and leaderboards. Apple processes that information under its own privacy policy. Your Season Points and fastest runs are posted to Game Center leaderboards, where other Game Center players can see them with your Game Center name.

Purchases are made through the App Store. Apple tells our service which product you bought and gives us a transaction ID so we can deliver it and handle refunds. We get no card or billing details.

Why we use it

  • To run the game you asked for: your profile, online parties, Friends, the Season Pass, the Shop and delivering purchases. This is necessary to provide the service (performance of a contract).
  • To keep players safe: checking names and chat, handling reports, blocks and suspensions, and stopping cheating and abuse. This is in our legitimate interest and yours in a fair, friendly game.
  • To keep records we're required to keep, such as purchase records for refunds and tax (legal obligation).
  • To run the Season Champion contest under its Official Rules.

We don't use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal effects.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your player name, #tag, runner and in-round activity. Mutual friends may see your playing status. Leaderboards show your Game Center name and score.
  • Cloudflare hosts the game service and its database for us. It processes data on our behalf and may keep its own request logs under its terms.
  • Apple provides Game Center and the App Store.
  • Netlify hosts this website.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We don't sell personal information and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, progress, wallet and items: until you delete your game profile.
  • Playing status: gone about a minute after you stop playing. No history is kept.
  • Friends hashes: replaced each time the game uploads them, and deleted after 30 days without an upload or when you turn off friends access.
  • Invitations: they expire after 5 minutes and are removed 24 hours after they're answered.
  • Closed parties: removed after 24 hours.
  • Typed chat: not stored, unless reported.
  • Reports and moderation records: as long as needed to keep the game safe and handle appeals.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed to honor purchases and refunds, as described below.

Deleting your profile

In the game, go to Settings › Account › Delete Game Profile. After you confirm, and Game Center confirms it's you, the game service deletes your profile, Game Center link, past names, blocks, friends hashes, playing status, invitations, wallet, items, progress and the text of reported messages you sent. The game then erases its data on your device.

A few records stay without any link to you: App Store transaction records (so a permanent App Store item can be restored to a new profile on the same Apple Account, and a consumable is never delivered twice), the Coins ledger, and reports you filed. Open reports about you are closed. Full details are on Delete your profile.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. You can change your name in the game and delete your profile yourself. For anything else, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your player name and #tag. We may need to confirm the profile is yours before acting. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have the rights described in the California Consumer Privacy Act; we don't sell or share personal information as that law defines it.

Children

Ultimate Trifecta isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the game service. If you believe a child under 13 has created an online profile, contact us and we'll delete it. Parents can limit multiplayer, friends and purchases with Screen Time and Game Center restrictions.

Security

All traffic between the game and the game service is encrypted (HTTPS). Sign-in is checked with Apple's Game Center signature, sessions are signed, friends lists are stored only as keyed hashes, and access to the database is restricted. No system is perfect, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if a breach affects you where the law requires it.

Where data is processed

Cloudflare runs the game service on servers in many countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts and videos are served from the site itself. Our hosting provider, Netlify, processes standard request information such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also say so in the game before it takes effect.
