{{SNOW}} Season 1

Winter Break

One hundred tiers, two tracks and a campus dressed for the holidays.

The Season Pass

Play rounds. Climb tiers.

Every online round earns Season XP, and so do the daily and weekly challenges. Each tier you reach unlocks a reward. Claim it and it's yours to keep.

  • Tiers100, with rewards all the way to the top.
  • Free track29 rewards for everyone: an outfit, hats, shoes, emotes, badges, name cards and 450 Coins.
  • Premium track44 more rewards, including five outfits and 1,125 Coins back.
  • Big tiersSignature outfits wait at tiers 50 and 100.

Premium

Unlock it once.

Premium opens the second track for all of Season 1. Get it in the game from the App Store, or with 1,500 Coins. Every Premium reward you've already earned becomes claimable straight away.

  • One purchaseNot a subscription. Nothing renews.
  • No shortcutsPremium doesn't skip tiers. You still play your way up.
  • No advantageSpeed, reach and score stay the same for everyone.
The Shop this winter

Dress for the cold.

Reindeer Onesie, Gingerbread Buddy, Elf Helper and Snow Day join the Shop rotation this winter, alongside Turkey Trot and Harvest Flannel. Buy with Coins you earn by playing, and save on full looks.
