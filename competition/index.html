Season 1 · Winter Break

Become the Season Champion.

No purchase necessary. The scheduled first season runs December 1 through December 30, 2026 (UTC), subject to launch and leaderboard availability.

Finish first in Season Points and pitch an original cosmetic outfit for a future Season Pass. The prize has no cash value.

Official rules

  1. Sponsor. The contest is run by Idlery Games, part of Idlery Services LLC, the developer of Ultimate Trifecta. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this contest in any way.
  2. No purchase necessary. Entry is free through eligible online rounds. Buying anything in the game does not improve your chances.
  3. Season. Each season runs 30 days (00:00 UTC on its first day through 23:59 UTC on its last day) and is a separate contest. Season 1 is scheduled for December 1 to December 30, 2026 UTC. The November preseason does not count.
  4. Winner selection. The player at #1 on the Season Points leaderboard in Game Center when the season ends wins, subject to eligibility and verification. Only online rounds with at least one other real player count. The developer reviews top scores and removes scores reasonably believed to have been cheated, made with a modified game or earned against these rules. The next eligible player moves up.
  5. Eligibility. Open to players with a Game Center account who are 13 or older (younger players need a parent or guardian's permission). Employees of the developer and their households cannot win. Void where prohibited by law.
  6. Prize. The winner may send the developer a drawing or description and a name for a cosmetic outfit. We will make an outfit inspired by it for the tier 100 reward of a following season's Season Pass, adapting it to the game's style and content rules. It must be suitable for all ages and must not copy another person's work, brand or likeness. The prize is cosmetic only, has no cash value and cannot be transferred or exchanged.
  7. Notification. The winner's Game Center name is announced in the game and on the support page when confirmed. The winner must contact us through support within 14 days or the next eligible player is chosen.
  8. Rights. By sending a concept, the winner confirms it is their own original work and allows the developer to use, adapt and publish it in the game and its promotion, with credit to the winner's chosen name.
  9. General. The developer may cancel or change the contest if it cannot run as planned, such as because of cheating or a technical failure, and will announce changes in the game. Questions may be sent to support@idlery.com.
