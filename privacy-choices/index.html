Privacy

Your data, your choice.

Manage your profile and online information in the game or contact us for help.

Delete your game profile

  1. Open Ultimate Trifecta.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Profile, then Delete Game Profile.
  4. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion.

If your game service is online, deletion removes your profile and associated game data from the service and device, subject to the purchase, refund and moderation record exceptions in our Privacy Policy. Deleting the app by itself does not delete your online profile. If the request fails or your device is unavailable, contact us for assistance.

Other privacy settings

  • Turn off Show when I'm playing in Friends to hide your current presence.
  • Manage Game Center friends and multiplayer permissions in your Apple settings.
  • Mute or block other players through their player cards where available.
  • Manage your Game Center data and Apple Account separately through Apple's privacy portal.

Request access, correction or deletion

Email us if you want to exercise a privacy right or need help deleting a profile. Include your game display name and tag, and the type of request. Never email your Apple password. We may need to verify you control the profile before processing a request.
