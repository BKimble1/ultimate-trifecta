Common questions

How do I play with friends?

Sign in to Game Center on your device, then use Friends to invite someone or join a party with a code. Both players need compatible game versions.

Can I play without internet?

Use Practice to play against bots offline. Online matches, rankings and service-backed rewards require a connection.

Why are Coins, rewards or some online features unavailable?

Some features require the online game service and App Store products to be enabled. During testing they may be unavailable. Do not attempt to buy an unavailable item.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Shop and choose Restore Purchases where available, using the original Apple Account. Previously delivered consumable Coins are not granted twice. For a refund, visit Apple's refund page.

How do I report or block someone?

Use the player card or message in the game and choose the available mute, report or block action. If the report service is unavailable, email us with the player's game name, approximate time and what happened. Never include sensitive personal information.

How do I delete my profile?

Open Settings, Profile, Delete Game Profile and confirm. For details or help if the online deletion fails, visit Privacy choices.

How do I enter the Season Champion competition?

Play eligible online rounds when a season is active. See the official rules. No purchase is necessary.

Player safety

To report harassment, inappropriate names or messages, cheating, or a safety concern, use the in-game reporting tools or email a safety report. The support team reviews reports and may take action under the community rules.
