How to play

Pick a side.

One campus. Two very different ways to win.

The runners

Find the three marked waters, take the shortcuts and make it back inside the dorm before time runs out. Get enough of your team home to win.

The Night Watch

Patrol the grounds, cut off the runners and tag them before they reach safety. On foot or behind the wheel of a golf cart.

Your night, your rules

Bring your friends.

Start a party, join Quick Match or practice offline with bots. Make your runner your own with pajamas, outfits and emotes.

Online play uses Apple Game Center. Some online features require the game service.
