Getting started

What do I need to play?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 17 or later and an A12 chip or newer. The game plays in landscape.

Do I need an account?

No separate account. The game uses Game Center, so sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings. The first time you play you'll pick a player name and make your runner.

Can I play on my own or offline?

Yes. Practice gives you full rounds against bots without an internet connection, as a runner, as the Night Watch or at random. There's a guided tutorial for each side too.

How does a round work?

Runners leave the dorm at GO, splash into the three waters marked for that round and race back inside through any door of their dorm. The Night Watch tries to stop them, on foot or in golf carts. A runner who is tagged is out for the round. Rounds last four minutes. Settings › About › How to play has the full rules card.

Does it work with a controller?

Yes. Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers work in the menus and in rounds. Touch controls can use a fixed or floating stick, with adjustable button size and a left-handed layout.

Playing with friends

How do I play with friends?

You're always in your own party lobby. Open Friends to invite Game Center friends, or share your party's six-character code so they can join with it. Up to eight players fit in a party, and bots fill any empty seats. Bots are always labelled BOT.

A friend doesn't show as online. Why?

Status only appears for friends who also have the game, have allowed friends access and list you as a Game Center friend too. Anyone can turn their own status off with "Show when I'm playing" at the end of the Friends list. Party codes and Game Center invites still work either way.

What is Quick Match?

Quick Match puts you in a round with other players online. If nobody is around after a short wait, the round starts with bots so you're never stuck waiting.

Who chooses the party settings?

The host picks one, two or three Night Watch and a series of one, three or five rounds, before the series starts. Roles are drawn fairly each round, and players who have had fewer turns as the Night Watch get the next ones.

Purchases and the Season Pass

What can I buy?

Coin packs, two permanent outfits and Season 1 Premium, all through the App Store. Coins buy outfits and accessories in the Shop. Everything is cosmetic: nothing you buy changes speed, reach or score.

I bought something and it didn't arrive.

Check that you're connected, then reopen the Shop. Purchases are confirmed with Apple and delivered once, even if the connection drops while you buy. If it still isn't there, email us with your player name and the date of the purchase from your Apple receipt.

How do I get my outfits back on a new device?

Sign in to the same Game Center account and your online profile comes with you. For items bought directly from the App Store, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. If a refund is granted, what it delivered is removed from your profile.

How does the Season Pass work?

Online rounds and daily and weekly challenges earn Season XP. Each tier you reach unlocks a reward on the free track, and on the Premium track if you have Premium. Tap Claim or Claim all to collect. Claimed rewards stay with your profile. See Season 1.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. It's a one-time unlock of the Premium track for Season 1, from the App Store or for 1,500 Coins. Nothing renews.

Safety

How do I mute, block or report someone?

Tap the player, or one of their chat messages, to mute, block or report them. Reports go to our moderation team with the context needed to act. A host can also remove a player from a party.

How is chat kept friendly?

Quick Chat phrases and emotes are always available. Typed chat goes through a filter that bleeps rude words however they're disguised and refuses links, phone numbers and handles. Player names are checked too. There's no voice chat.

Parental controls

The game follows Screen Time and Game Center restrictions on multiplayer games and friends. In-app purchases can be limited in Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions.

Your profile and data

How do I change my player name?

Go to Settings › Account › Profile. New names are checked before they're used.

How do I delete my game profile?

Settings › Account › Delete Game Profile. Here's exactly what it removes.

What data does the game keep?

Only what it needs to run your profile, purchases, parties and safety tools. No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. The Privacy Policy lists everything.
