The rules

  1. Sponsor

    This contest is run by Idlery Games, the developer of Ultimate Trifecta. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this contest in any way.

  2. No purchase necessary

    Entry is free: you take part by playing online rounds. Buying anything in the game does not improve your chances.

  3. Season

    Season 1 · Winter Break runs from 2026-12-01 00:00 UTC to 2026-12-30 23:59 UTC (30 days). Each season is a separate contest.

  4. How the winner is decided

    The player at #1 on the Season Points leaderboard in Game Center when the season ends. Season Points come only from online rounds with at least one other real player (Rankings explains how points are earned). Before announcing a winner, the developer reviews the top scores and removes any it reasonably believes were cheated, made with a modified game, or earned against these rules; the next eligible player then moves up.

  5. Eligibility

    Open to players with a Game Center account who are 13 or older (younger players need a parent or guardian's permission). Employees of the developer and their households can't win. Void where prohibited by law.

  6. The prize

    The winner may send the developer a concept (a drawing, a description or both) and a name for a cosmetic outfit. The developer will make an outfit inspired by it, as the tier 100 reward of a following season's Season Pass, adapting it to the game's style and content rules (it must be suitable for all ages and must not copy anyone else's work, brand or likeness). The prize has no cash value, can't be transferred and can't be exchanged. The outfit is cosmetic only.

  7. Contact

    The developer announces the winner's Game Center name in the game and on its support page; the winner contacts the developer through the support page within 14 days, or the next eligible player is chosen.

  8. Rights

    By sending a concept the winner confirms it is their own original work and allows the developer to use, adapt and publish it in the game and its promotion, with credit to the winner's chosen name.

  9. General

    The developer may cancel or change the contest if it can't run as planned (for example because of cheating or a technical failure), and will say so in the game. Questions: the support page in Settings.

How Season Points are earned

Each online round with at least one other real player adds points to your season total, as the results screen shows after every round.

ForPoints
Playing the round10
Your side wins15
Each splash, as a runner (up to 3)5
Making it home, as a runner10
First runner home5 more
Each different runner tagged, as the Night Watch (up to 3)10

A round counts for at most 70 points, and up to 40 rounds count each day. Practice rounds and rounds with only bots don't count. There are no hidden ratings, and points never affect gameplay.

Season dates

Seasons last 30 days. Season 1 · Winter Break runs from December 1, 2026, 00:00 UTC to December 30, 2026, 23:59 UTC. The November preseason has no leaderboard and no champion.
