Delete your game profile

You can delete your profile and its data from inside the game at any time. It takes a few seconds and can't be undone.

On this page

  1. In the game
  2. What's removed
  3. What's kept
  4. App Store purchases
  5. Without the game

In the game

  1. Open Ultimate Trifecta, signed in to the Game Center account the profile belongs to.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear at the top right).
  3. On the Account tab, scroll to Delete game profile and tap Delete Game Profile.
  4. Read what will be removed and tap Delete. Game Center confirms it's you, the online profile is deleted, and the game erases its data on your device and starts fresh.

If the connection fails, nothing is removed and the game offers to try again.

What's removed

  • Your online profile: player name and #tag, past names and your runner's look.
  • The link between your Game Center account and the profile.
  • Coins (earned and bought), items bought with Coins, Season 1 progress, Premium access granted with Coins, claimed rewards and challenge progress.
  • Your block list, your friends hashes, your playing status and your invitations.
  • The text of any reported chat messages you sent.
  • On your device: your name, runner, level, stats, settings, block list and cached Coins and items.

What's kept, without your profile

A few records stay so the game can keep its promises to you and to other players. They no longer point to any profile:

  • App Store transaction records (transaction ID, product and environment), so a refund can be honored, a consumable is never delivered twice, and a permanent App Store item can come back to a new profile on the same Apple Account.
  • The Coins ledger.
  • Reports you filed about other players. Open reports about you are closed.
  • Moderation logs record that a profile was deleted, without its ID.

Game Center leaderboard entries belong to your Game Center account and are managed by Apple.

App Store purchases

Deleting your profile doesn't cancel or refund anything with Apple. Items bought directly from the App Store, the two permanent outfits and Season 1 Premium, stay with your Apple Account. On a new profile, open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases to get them back. Coins and Coin-bought items can't be restored.

If you can't open the game

Email support@idlery.com with the subject "Delete my game profile". Include your player name with its #tag and your Game Center nickname. We'll confirm the profile is yours before deleting it, then let you know when it's done. We aim to complete requests within 30 days.
